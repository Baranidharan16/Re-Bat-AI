Lifetime model

Runs this battery day after day through Chennai's seasons (15-minute steps) until it reaches end of second life. Three charging strategies, same measured pack.

Ageing coefficients are literature-order values for aged NMC/LFP, not fitted to this pack. Re-measure capacity on the rig every few months and enter it here to recalibrate. CO₂ uses India's grid factor of about 0.71 kg/kWh.
